description: 'A quick guide on trunk-based development and why teams use it.'
pubDate: 'Sep 16 2026'
author: 'claiton'
---

Trunk-based development is a version control strategy where developers integrate small, frequent changes into a single main branch instead of maintaining long-lived feature branches.

This approach reduces merge conflicts and keeps the codebase always close to a releasable state.
